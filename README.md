# Structural Limits of Optimization in Human-Centered Systems

This repository contains:

1. **Research Paper:** "Structural Limits of Optimization in Human-Centered Systems"  
   - Synthesizes prior research on predictive systems, anomalies, and human agency.  
   - Highlights how optimization frameworks misinterpret principled behavior and the structural consequences of trust erosion.  
   - Discusses integrity as an unmodelable boundary signal that preserves human agency.

2. **Python Simulation:**  
   - Models agents acting with integrity or opportunism.  
   - Tracks trust erosion across a network over time.  
   - Demonstrates how predictive or optimization-driven systems would perceive and react to principled deviations.  
   - Illustrates structural consequences of misaligned incentive systems.

---

## Key Concepts

- **Integrity vs Opportunism:** Agents may follow stated principles or seek short-term gains.  
- **Trust Erosion:** Repeated misalignment between words and actions reduces perceived reliability.  
- **Network Effects:** Trust dynamics propagate across interacting agents.  
- **Anomalies as Signals:** Deviations from expected behavior indicate model limitations, not failures of the agent.  
- **Structural Sovereignty:** Human agency persists in ways that cannot be fully captured or optimized by predictive systems.
